# LUMINA Editorial Framework
## Executive Brief & Operational Scope
**Status:** Production Ready · **Version:** v3.0.0-RC2 · **Scope:** Reusable, Expandible, Reference-First

---

## What LUMINA Is

LUMINA is a **portable, layered editorial metasystem** that turns literary voice into scalable production. It's not a platform—it's a **schema, pattern set, and operational scaffold** for:

- **Literary diagnostics** (Mapa de Voz: 5D voice profiling)
- **Editorial governance** (BOS→ÆQUITAS→LUMINA→OPAL stacking engine)
- **Transmedia delivery** (interactive HTML, print-ready DOCX/PDF, browser workbooks)
- **Brand identity** (color tokens, typography, tone, spacing rules — all derived from single source)

**Core assumption:** Every author has a voice. Every voice has a measurable structure. That structure scales into production workflows.
